FreestyleParser — Help 🌐 Русский

The program splits freestyle kayak recordings into individual attempts and recognizes which athlete performed each one. Everything you need is in the left column: Files → Region of interest → Processing settings.

Contents: Quick start · Files · Region of interest · Processing settings · Slow motion · Strategies · Athletes and banks · View without saving · Scan · Video player · Hotkeys · Logs · Tiles · FAQ

Quick start

  1. Click 📅 and pick a day folder (clips, screenshots and markup files will be created there).
  2. Add the source videos with Add files in the «Files» panel.
  3. In the «Region of interest» panel, outline the zone where the kayaker performs tricks: «Rectangle» or «Polygon» mode, double-click finishes the polygon.
  4. If the camera recorded slow motion (96 fps on a Panasonic GH4) — enable Slow motion and pick the factor.
  5. Pick a strategy and press ⏵ Process.
  6. After cutting, assign attempts to athletes on the Athletes tab — from then on the program will recognize them automatically.

Files

This is where the source videos of the day are selected. Files shown in red are missing on disk (e.g. the memory card is already unplugged). The selected list is saved in the day folder and restored when you reopen it. The first file is shown on the left — it is used for ROI markup.

Region of interest

The program only watches the zone inside the ROI — tricks are performed in the feature bowl on the wave. Two modes:

  • Rectangle — click twice (top-left and bottom-right corner); the sides can be dragged.
  • Polygon — click the points, double-click finishes the shape.

«Clear» resets the ROI. The markup is stored in the day config.

Processing settings

All parameters are stored in the day config (processing.yaml) and restored when the day is opened — each day keeps its own settings. The defaults suit typical 25–30 fps footage with fast entries into the feature bowl.

ParameterDefaultWhat it does
Pause between attempts2 sMinimum pause after which two entries into the ROI count as different attempts. Lower — attempts split; higher — neighboring entries merge into one.
Min. attempt duration2 sShorter attempts are discarded. Lower — more junk entries; higher — risk of losing short tricks.
Start padding1.5 sHow many seconds before the ROI entry are included in the clip (preroll) — so the approach is visible.
End padding1.5 sHow many seconds after leaving the ROI are appended to the clip (glide-out).
Min. detection strength0.3How «confidently» the athlete must be present in the ROI for the start/continuation of an attempt to count (0–1). Higher — fewer false positives, but fast/distant entries may be missed.
Scan step, s1 s (choices: 1/2, 1, 2)Sampling step when scanning for candidates (manual mode and fast «👁 View»). Lower — sharper boundaries, a slower scan. If the file has I-frames sparser than the step, a warning with a recommended step appears in the log.
Scan threshold0.25YOLO detection threshold during scanning (0–1) — deliberately below the normal analysis threshold so entries at the ROI border are not missed.
OSNet samples/s per attempt3How many vest embeddings per real second are taken inside an attempt for attribution. More — better recognition, slower processing. 1–2 is enough in good light.
Search start to the left, s3.5 sHow far to the left of a candidate the precise splitter searches for the true attempt start (precise attribution / «Detect athletes»).
Start with the defaults. If attempts merge — increase «Pause between attempts»; if the program catches kayakers passing by — increase «Min. detection strength» or shrink the ROI.

Slow motion

High-resolution cameras often record «slow motion»: 96 fps captured, but the container is labeled 24 fps. Enable Slow motion and pick the factor:

FactorRecording
1/248 fps in a 24 fps container
1/496 fps (Panasonic GH4) — the typical case
1/8192 fps

With slow motion enabled: attempt timings are in real seconds, cut clips are saved at normal (watchable) speed. For VFR files (r_frame_rate higher than avg, progressive) the factor is detected automatically; for the Panasonic GH4 slow motion is baked into container timestamps and cannot be told from metadata — enable the checkbox manually. Interlaced 1080/50i footage is not slow motion and is automatically treated as normal speed.

Strategies

Save immediatelyDetect athletesResult
☑☐All detected attempts are cut (no attribution).
☐☐Preview candidates for manual selection.
☐☑Athlete scan: segments + attribution in the originals, no cutting.
☑☑Full cycle: cutting + athlete assignment.

Candidate scanning always uses keyframes (I-frames) only: minimal decoding, fast even from a flash drive. Samples are taken from I-frames at the «Scan step, s» interval (if I-frames are sparser than the step, all of them are used: 0.125 s GOP on GoPro slow-mo → one sample per second; 2 s GOP → all I-frames). If I-frames are on average sparser than ~5 s, the scan automatically falls back to regular samples and reports it in the log. If I-frames are sparser than the chosen scan step, a warning and a step recommendation appear in the log. Scan speed is also visible in the log heartbeat (file position and samples/s every 30 s) and in the ETA under the progress bar.

Athletes and banks

On the Athletes tab every cut attempt is a tile with a frame. Under the tile: the athlete selector (a colored chip) and a confirm button:

  • Orange chip — automatic detection (with a confidence percentage). Check it and press ✓NN% to confirm.
  • Green chip — confirmed manually.
  • Grey «— select —» — athlete not determined; pick one from the list or via «➕ New / search…» (with live suggestions; the 🔍 magnifier searches by substring).

Every confirmation adds the attempt profile to the athlete's bank. Bank layers (a closer layer overrides an athlete of the same name):

  1. Base banks — ship with the program (e.g. RussianCup2026, collected at the Russian Cup), read-only.
  2. Personal registry — confirmations from all your days (~/.FreestyleParser/athlete_registry.npz).
  3. Day folder — assignments in the current folder (mapping.yaml).

Matching is max over bank samples: an athlete is recognized in a different jacket and a different season if at least one sample matches. A match requires both vest similarity (OSNet) and boat shape agreement.

Delete a tile with the 🗑 button on the tile or in the player (press twice to protect from accidental deletion). To delete an empty athlete — ✕ next to their name on the «Athletes» tab.

Scan

The Scan tab (in the «Athletes / Scan» panel) shows segments found in the originals without cutting: who entered the ROI, when, and who they are according to the bank. Useful for getting a picture of the day before full processing. The bank status shows how many athletes are available for recognition.

View without saving

The 👁 View button scans the selected files without writing anything to disk: found attempts appear as in-memory tiles and can be watched in the built-in player right away.

  • Detect athletes off — fast coarse scan (samples once per «Scan step, s»); on — precise splitter (slow).
  • In the player, the 💾 Save buttons / keys S (current) and A (previous) mark an attempt: a checkbox appears in the panel and the button shows «⏳ queued».
  • Writing to disk happens in a batch after the player is closed — only the marked attempts. Nothing marked — nothing saved.
This is a safe way to review a shooting day: the disk stays clean, and only the attempts you marked go into cutting.

Video player

Double-click a tile to open the built-in player:

  • Click the video — pause/play; drag the video — move the window.
  • ☐ Repeat — loop the clip.
  • ☐ Continue viewing — when the clip ends, the next attempt opens automatically in the same window. It also works for candidates (viewing from the source): when an attempt ends, the next candidate opens; Shift+arrows browse them manually.
  • Athlete selector — the same chip as on the tile; choosing/confirming updates the tile immediately.
  • 🗑 Delete — two-phase: the first click colors the button, the second deletes the clip (with the screenshot and all assignments).

Logs

The Logs panel (opened with the ▸ Logs button in the bottom-left corner) shows what is going on: detected attempts with their boundaries, attribution, processing errors and interface messages. Look here when something goes wrong: the program writes why an attempt was skipped or not cut. The same text is written to files: processing.log in the day folder and ~/.FreestyleParser/app.log (all sessions). Copy the panel text with the 📋 Copy all button or a right-click on the text.

Attempt tiles

Tiles are laid out across the panel width — the number of columns adjusts to the window size automatically.

FAQ

The program doesn't find attempts

Check the ROI (the zone must cover the whole wave with the feature bowl) and the «Pause between attempts» / «Min. attempt duration» / «Min. detection strength» parameters. Try the «preview candidates» strategy (both checkboxes off) — it shows what the program sees at all — and lower the «Scan step» for denser sampling.

An athlete is recognized incorrectly

Confirm the correct attempts — the day folder mapping overrides the base bank. If the athlete is new (not in the bank), they will appear as «— select —», and after 2–3 confirmations they will start being recognized.

A 96 fps video was treated as normal speed

GH4 slow motion is baked into the timestamps; metadata doesn't differ. Enable «Slow motion» with factor 1/4 manually.

Processing was interrupted (laptop sleep, restart)

Just press «⏵ Process» again: finished files of the day are skipped, the unfinished file continues where it stopped, and already-cut attempts are not duplicated (the attempts journal in the day folder). Clip numbering continues without gaps. To deliberately re-process a file, remove its entries from processed.yaml and attempts_index.yaml in the day folder (the files are human-readable).

Where is the data stored

Everything for a day lives in the day folder (clips, jpg, mapping.yaml, attempt_profiles.npz). The personal registry and base banks are in ~/.FreestyleParser/. Base banks are never modified by the program.
